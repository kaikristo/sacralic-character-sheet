# Sacralic ability audit

Audited 2026-10-18 against the connected Sacralic spreadsheet (modified 2026-10-15). Catalog: 31 simple feats, 19 epic feats, 44 prestige classes, 14 melee moves.

The table distinguishes numeric modifiers, explicit player actions, and GM decisions. An entry appearing here does not mean that narrative events, enemies, pets, vehicle state, target defenses, critical damage, or campaign rules are simulated automatically. Full source rules remain available in every card.

## Automated regression checks

Run `npm test`. The ability suite covers every catalog entry for configuration, disabling, valid effects, rule text and rendering, with behavioral checks for point grants/idempotence, repeat targets, skill caps, weapon/AP/mastery modifiers, critical modifiers, armor, HP/EP, psychic restrictions and regeneration, dual weapons, move prerequisites/AP, combinations, stances/expiry/undo and lifetime counters. Existing effects tests cover passive feat/class interactions. Firestore integration remains a separate deployment gate.

## Melee moves

All 14 source moves appear in the dedicated picker for both melee styles, with prerequisites and the learned-move budget shown. Execution checks ownership of the learned move, the selected melee skill, prerequisites (or explicit GM override), and AP. It displays the current chance, damage, armor exception and target defense. Stances apply their modifiers and expire; flurry ends on movement or round end. Combination rolls its two selected effects independently and requires the GM to specify its cost because the source lists no cost. Target anatomy, natural/special armor, range, defense rolls, stun/death effects, and multi-target selection still require GM resolution.

## Online tactics boundary

Import copies passive HP/AP/movement, weapon damage/BB/chances, prestige armor and effective initiative. First Turn is respected when sorting teams. Temporary sheet stances and flurry are cleared on import so they do not become permanent battle bonuses. The tactical engine still resolves basic attacks; active prestige/feat actions, melee moves, critical effects, pets, auras and enemy reactions must be resolved through the sheet and host controls. They are not claimed as automatic online mechanics.

## Existing saves

Education/Master/Talent had no automatic point grants before this audit. For existing saves the app preserves the current point banks and offers a review in the ability panel: mark old grants as already accounted for, or issue missing points once. New selections grant points automatically and store a ledger. Removing a feat reverses its grant and may produce a negative point bank. Existing manual stat/skill bonuses are preserved.
